# Buddy Scout — VISION (North Star)

> This is the destination. Every change should bend toward it.
> Written 2026-09-12 with Tomi. The engine (buyer names, cities, hiring signal)
> is built. This is the steering wheel: how that data becomes *replies in Jolene's inbox*.

## The reframe
Buddy Scout is **not a research tool. It's a "get the meeting" machine.**
Jolene doesn't want information. She wants a reply from Winston at Harvey.
Everything bends toward that one outcome. Today it's a beautiful dossier.
The product is a **decision + an action**: who to hit, what to say, words already written.

## The five moves

### 1. Lead with a VERDICT, not data
First thing on the card is a call, not a bio:
**"Chase this. Hit Winston Weinberg today — 40 GTM roles in 2 weeks, peak screening pain, and he feels it."**
Go / Watch / Skip. Research lives *underneath* for verification. Make the decision, show the work below.

### 2. Commit to ONE person + ONE reason
Not a menu of 2-3 buyers (that's homework). Pick one, name why:
**"Winston, CEO — at their stage he owns hiring and feels every bad hire."**
Confidence is decisiveness.

### 3. The message is a HOOK, not a pitch
Two curious sentences engineered to earn a reply. Their pain, named. No "Buddy," no features:
**"Winston — you've posted 40 GTM roles in two weeks. How's your team screening that volume without it eating everyone's week?"**
Buddy comes up in message 2, after he replies. Message 1's only job: earn message 2.

### 4. A SEQUENCE, not a shot
Hook → (no reply, 3d) → soft value → (no reply) → clean breakup. All three pre-written.
Reps live on follow-up; the product owns the rhythm.

### 5. Close the loop — it LEARNS
She tags each: replied / meeting / dead. Over time it learns which hooks get *Jolene* replies
and writes more of those. Becomes her outreach coach that sharpens weekly.
**This is the moat — not the data, the feedback loop.**

## The experience, end to end
She pastes `harvey.ai`. Three seconds later:
*"Chase it. Winston Weinberg, CEO, San Francisco. Here's your first message — copy it."*
She reads two lines, thinks "that's exactly what I'd want to be asked," copies, sends.
3 days later: *"No reply from Winston — here's your follow-up."*
2 weeks later: *"Your 'how are you screening that volume' hook is getting 40% replies. Leaning in."*

## Build order
1. **Verdict line + hook-first draft** — biggest felt difference. Rewrite of Draft + a header. FIRST.
2. **Commit to one buyer** with one sharp reason (uses data we have).
3. **3-step sequence.**
4. **Feedback loop** — makes it hers, compounds.

## Why it's achievable
The expensive parts are done: real buyer names, cities, live hiring signal.
This is reshaping presentation + writing: a verdict, a single-buyer commit, a hook,
a sequence, one feedback field. Prompt + structure, not a new engine.
